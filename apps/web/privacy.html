Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

Welcome to CompareAll. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a safe online experience. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices.

1. Data Collection and Storage

We do NOT collect, transmit, or store your personal data on any external servers.

All data processed by CompareAll, including your search queries, location, platform logins (e.g., Swiggy, Zomato, EatSure), and cart details, is strictly processed and stored locally on your device.

2. Third-Party Services

CompareAll functions as a comparison tool and interacts directly with third-party food delivery platforms via secure internal web views. When you log in to these platforms through CompareAll, your credentials and session data are stored securely within the local sandbox of your device and are never sent to CompareAll servers.

Please refer to the privacy policies of the respective third-party platforms (Swiggy, Zomato, EatSure, etc.) for information on how they handle your data once you are interacting with their services.

3. Location Data

We may ask for location permissions to help fetch real-time and accurate food prices for your area. This location data is processed locally on your device to query the respective food delivery platforms and is never collected or tracked by us.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Since we do not collect your contact information, we encourage you to review this page periodically for any changes.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@compareall.in.
